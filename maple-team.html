House rules

Three laws the team lives by

Ask first, always The team never auto-engages. On a coding task the orchestrator asks “Use Maple Team for this?” — no yes, no team.
Agents can't call agents Only the main thread routes. It carries the plan to the engineer and the diff to QA — every hand-off passes through the middle.
Model is fixed at spawn An agent can't change its own brain mid-run. Need more horsepower? Route to a different agent — that's why two engineers exist.
Lineage

Named for a guild of eight

Maple Team takes its name from Guild Maple Tree — the tiny guild from the anime BOFURI: I Don't Want to Get Hurt, so I'll Max Out My Defense. A handful of members, each specialized to an absurd degree, beating guilds fifty times their size through preparation and one stubborn principle: refuse to take damage.

Same philosophy here. A small roster of extreme specialists, and QA as the maxed-out defense — nothing ships that can hurt you.
